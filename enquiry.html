Frequently Asked Questions

Our stores are open Monday to Friday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and Saturdays from 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM. We're closed on Sundays and public holidays.

Currently, we only ship within South Africa. We're working on expanding our shipping options in the near future.

We offer a 14-day return policy for most items. Products must be in original condition with all packaging and accessories. Some items like software and personal care products may have different return policies.

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can use this number on our website's order tracking page or the courier's website to follow your package.
